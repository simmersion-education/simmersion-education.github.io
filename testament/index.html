Testament

Testament is inner space exploration: a reflective world where learners step into the parables and return seeing their own lives more clearly.

What is Testament?

Testament invites learners to cross the threshold into the world of the parables. Not as observers, but as participants inside a living narrative. Each mission offers a moment of encounter, reflection, and perspective‑taking. It is not about information. It is about transformation.

Current Status

Testament is in prototype form. Early scenarios explore compassion, conflict, responsibility, and self‑understanding through immersive narrative experience.
