Testament

Testament develops moral formation and narrative intelligence through immersive parable‑based missions.

What is Testament?

Testament invites learners to step into modernised parables — not as observers, but as participants. Missions explore empathy, perspective, character, and moral reasoning through lived narrative experience.

Current Status

Testament is in prototype form. Early scenarios explore decision‑making, compassion, conflict, and responsibility through narrative immersion.
