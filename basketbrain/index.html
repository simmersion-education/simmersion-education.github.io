BasketBrain

BasketBrain is a business and operational simulation that trains practical decision‑making.

What is BasketBrain?

BasketBrain places learners inside the operational pressures of running a supermarket: stock levels, waste, demand, queues, labour, deliveries, substitutions, and more. It builds operational awareness and resource‑management skills.

Data Engine (In Production)

BasketBrain will include a dedicated data engine, similar in architecture to the Nexus behaviour engine. This will model stock flows, perishables, pricing, and operational constraints.
