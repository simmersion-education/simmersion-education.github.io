BasketBrain

BasketBrain is consumer space exploration: a fast, playful world where learners step inside the pressures and decisions of real retail.

What is BasketBrain?

BasketBrain places learners inside the moving parts of a supermarket: stock levels, waste, demand, queues, labour, deliveries, substitutions, and more. It builds operational awareness, practical judgement, and real‑time decision‑making in a world that feels alive.

Data Engine (In Production)

BasketBrain includes a dedicated data engine that models stock flows, perishables, pricing, demand curves, and operational constraints. It is designed to create realistic scenarios that challenge learners to think clearly under pressure.
