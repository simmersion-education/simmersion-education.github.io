Nexus

Nexus is outer space exploration: a mission‑driven world where learners practise systems thinking, forecasting, and calm decision‑making under pressure.

What is Nexus?

Nexus places learners inside a living sci‑fi environment where every decision shapes the mission. It helps people explore complex systems, recognise patterns, and respond to shifting scenarios with clarity and confidence. Nexus is not a game. It is a training ground for structured thinking.

Current Nexus Modules

  • GeoEngineering Forecast Room (Solo) — record forecasts, listen back, and receive structured feedback.
  • Observation Deck (Prototype) — explore the full holodeck map and step through the interactive mission loop.

The Behaviour Engine

Nexus includes a working behaviour engine that tracks system metrics, crew stress, anomalies, and scenario tension. This engine makes missions feel alive, reactive, and meaningful.
