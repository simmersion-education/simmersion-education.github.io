Envoy

Envoy develops communication and relational intelligence through social strategy missions.

What is Envoy?

Envoy helps learners practise clarity, empathy, negotiation, and perspective‑taking. Missions place learners in dynamic social situations where they must read cues, manage tone, and navigate competing priorities.

Current Status

Envoy is in early development. Scenario templates, communication loops, and feedback structures are being prototyped.
