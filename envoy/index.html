Envoy

Envoy is human space exploration: a world of live social scenarios that build clarity, empathy, and relational intelligence.

What is Envoy?

Envoy places learners inside dynamic human situations where tone, timing, and perspective matter. Missions help learners practise reading cues, managing tension, repairing misunderstandings, and communicating with confidence. It is a safe, structured space to grow the skills that shape real conversations.

Current Status

Envoy is in early development. Scenario templates, communication loops, and feedback structures are being prototyped and tested.
